Field to Package

Our rice process

01

Plowing

The soil is plowed to loosen the earth, turn over weeds naturally, and create a flat surface for planting.

02

Seedling

Seedlings are grown in nursery beds for around 30–50 days before being transplanted by hand.

03

Planting

Each seedling is planted one by one by hand at carefully measured points across the field.

04

Irrigation

A shallow layer of water supports healthy growth, regulates soil temperature, and naturally suppresses weeds.

05

Weeding & fertilizing

Weeds are removed by hand so nutrients go to the rice. Natural fertilizers support healthy growth.

06

Pest control

Pak Putu makes handmade tools and works with nature — even welcoming owls — to drive pests away without harm.

07

Harvesting

Once the plants turn golden and fully mature, the rice is carefully harvested.

08

Drying

The rice is dried under the sun or by machine until it reaches the ideal moisture level to preserve quality.

09

Milling

The rice is milled to remove the outer husk while preserving the flavor of each grain.

10

Packaging & sealing

Each package is filled, sealed, and prepared by hand to ensure freshness before reaching customers.
